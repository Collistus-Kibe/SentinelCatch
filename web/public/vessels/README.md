# SentinelCatch Real Vessel Photography Archive

SentinelCatch displays authentic, verified maritime surveillance photography streamed directly from open databases:
1. **Trygg Mat Tracking (TMT) / Combined IUU Fishing Vessel List** (`https://iuu-vessels.org`) for RFMO blacklisted dark vessels.
2. **Wikimedia Commons Open Maritime Fleet** (`https://commons.wikimedia.org`) for commercial reefers, container carriers, longliners, and trawlers under Creative Commons.
3. **VesselFinder & MyShipTracking Open Registries**.

No synthetic or AI-generated mockup images are stored or served from this directory.
